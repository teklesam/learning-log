# 🩺🤖 learning-log

A daily, public log of how I'm learning to build and evaluate **clinical AI**, written from the point of view of a doctor who wants to understand models well enough to know which ones belong in a clinic.

**Who I am:** Samuel Tekle. Medical doctor (Eritrea, 2021): GP, then medical director at Massawa Hospital. MPhil in Health Data Science, University of Cambridge. Now a Research Associate at the University of Oxford (Oxford Martin Programme on Climate & Child Health) and an ML engineering intern in clinical imaging.

**Where I'm heading:** clinician-academic in **clinical imaging AI**, with a focus on cardiology and neurology, and on making models **fair, interpretable and honestly evaluated**.

## Rules I write by
1. **One entry per day.** Even 20 minutes counts.
2. **Honest:** what I understood, *and* what I didn't.
3. **Public-safe:** no employer code or data, no patient data, nothing under embargo.

## Structure
```
2026/

  week-01/   ...
```
Each entry has four parts: **Did · Understood · Didn't understand · Next**.
